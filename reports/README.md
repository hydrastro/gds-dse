# Research outputs

Keep selected small tables, figures and interpretation here.

Each reported result should identify its campaign labels, study commit,
experiment definition and raw-data archive with a checksum. Explain the sample
size, failure handling, metric population and operating targets.

Complete flow logs, GDS, DEF, SPEF and frozen campaign inputs belong in a separate
research archive. The ignored `results/` directory is a working location, not
a backup.

Generated tables are evidence for a conclusion; they are not a substitute for
writing down what was measured and what the result supports.
